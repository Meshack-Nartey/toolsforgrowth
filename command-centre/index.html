FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know Excel formulas?

No. You only type into the blue cells; every grey cell calculates itself. The Quick Start guide walks you through setup.

What do I need to use it?

Microsoft Excel 2010 or later on Windows or Mac is recommended. It also opens in LibreOffice Calc. It's designed for a computer; the Excel phone app is fine for quick entries.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

It was built and tested for Excel and LibreOffice. Google Sheets wasn't tested, so we can't guarantee charts or sheet protection there.

Can I use my own currency?

Yes. Set your currency code and symbol (for example GHS / GH₵, NGN / ₦, GBP / £) in Settings. The workbook doesn't convert between currencies.

Can it handle customers who pay in instalments?

Yes. Record each instalment as a separate payment. The order shows PART-PAID until it's fully paid.

How many products and orders can it hold?

150 products, 300 customers, 500 orders, 1,000 order lines and 1,000 payments per workbook. These limits were tested at full capacity.

How do I get it after paying?

Checkout is handled by Selar. You get a download link immediately after payment, and by email.

Will it do my taxes?

No. There's an optional estimated tax percentage for a rough figure only. Speak to a qualified professional about tax.
