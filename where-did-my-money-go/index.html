FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. The guide explains everything in plain English with examples, and the spreadsheet shows you exactly which cells to type in.

Can I use it in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload it to Google Drive and choose File → Save as Google Sheets. Formulas, dropdowns and charts carry over; Google Sheets doesn't keep Excel's sheet protection.

Can I use it on my phone?

The PDFs open on any phone and the spreadsheet works in the free Google Sheets or Excel app. The dashboard and planners are easier on a tablet or computer.

Is this a physical book?

No. It's a digital download: PDF files and a spreadsheet. You can print the planners at home for your own use.
