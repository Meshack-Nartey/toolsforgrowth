Last updated: 14 November 2026

This website

This website is a simple information site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms or accounts. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) to run and secure its service; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (for example, which pages are viewed, roughly which country visits come from, and whether a visit led to a purchase). Google Analytics sets cookies and processes this information on Google's behalf; see Google's privacy policy. We also measure whether our Google Ads lead to visits and purchases. We use this only to improve the site and our advertising, never to identify you personally.

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. If you decline, Google Analytics does not set cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the cookie settings link.

Purchases

When you buy, you complete checkout on Selar. Selar collects the details needed to process your payment and deliver your files, under its own privacy policy. We receive order details such as your name and email so we can support you. We don't sell your information.

Our products

Our spreadsheets work offline on your own device. Anything you type into them stays on your device unless you choose to share it.
